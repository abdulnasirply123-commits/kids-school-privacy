Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

🛡️

Kid-Safe & Privacy Focused

KIDS SCHOOL is built with children's safety and privacy as our highest priority. We do NOT collect, store, sell, or share personal information from our users. The app is safe, works offline for all learning content, and complies with Google Play Families Policies.

📖 Introduction

Welcome to KIDS SCHOOL, an educational application operated by KIDS SCHOOL ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you download and use our app on the Google Play Store.

KIDS SCHOOL is designed to provide a fun, safe, and engaging learning environment for toddlers, preschoolers, and young children to learn alphabets, numbers, colors, shapes, animals, fruits, and basic math.

We encourage parents, guardians, and teachers to review this policy together with the children in their care. By downloading or using KIDS SCHOOL, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

🔍 Information We Collect

KIDS SCHOOL does NOT collect any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No Account Required: Children and parents can use the entire app without creating an account, registering, or logging in.
  • No Personal Details: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, ages, birthdates, phone numbers, photos, or physical addresses.
  • No Location Data: We do not access, track, or store your GPS or network location.
  • No Device Sensors or Media: We do not access your camera, microphone, photo gallery, or device files.

⚙️ How We Use Information

Since we do not collect any personal data, no personal information is stored, processed, or transmitted to any server.

  • Local Gameplay & Progress: Educational quiz scores, sound preferences, and language settings are stored purely locally on your device storage.
  • Offline Operation: All educational lessons, voice audios, pictures, and quizzes run directly on your device without requiring an active internet connection.
  • No Ads: KIDS SCHOOL does not display any advertisements and contains no third-party advertising SDKs. The app makes no network requests during normal use.

🤝 Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any personal information with third-party companies, advertisers, or organizations. The only situations in which information may be shared are:

  • Distribution Platforms: Google Play Store processes standard installation, crash, and device information according to Google's Privacy Policy .
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect the safety and rights of our users or the public.

We never disclose personal information for monetary or other valuable consideration unless you provide it to us voluntarily, for example through a support email.

👶 Children's Privacy

KIDS SCHOOL is designed for children and follows applicable child privacy requirements, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), applicable GDPR-K requirements, and Google Play Families policies.

  • We do not knowingly collect or request personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union where applicable).
  • There are no online chat rooms, social media integrations, direct messaging, or communication channels with other users.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that any personal information has inadvertently been provided, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

🔒 Data Storage and Security

Because KIDS SCHOOL does not transfer personal data to external servers or cloud databases, personal app data is not stored on our servers. Local app preferences, such as sound settings or language selection, are stored within the app on your device.

For any information you choose to share with us directly, such as through support email, we apply reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect that information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

🧩 Third-Party Services

KIDS SCHOOL does not use any third-party services that collect personal information.

  • No Ads, No Ad SDKs: KIDS SCHOOL does not display advertisements and does not integrate any advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • Local Text-to-Speech: Voice pronunciations use your device's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine locally on the device.
  • External Browser: When you open this Privacy Policy from inside the app, it is displayed in your device's default web browser. No personal data from the app is shared when doing so.

🍪 Cookies and Analytics

Cookies: KIDS SCHOOL is a native mobile application and does not use browser cookies. No cookies are set or stored by the app itself.

Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics services such as Google Analytics for Firebase to collect personal information about children.

⏳ Data Retention

  • On-Device Data: Because KIDS SCHOOL stores settings and progress locally on the device, no such data is retained on our servers. You can delete this data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.
  • Support Correspondence: If you contact us by email, we retain that correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and resolve your request.

We do not intentionally retain personal information longer than necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

👨‍👩‍👧 User & Parental Rights

Parents and guardians retain control over their children's use of the app.

  • Access and Control: You may manage or delete locally stored app data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling KIDS SCHOOL.
  • Deletion: If you believe personal information has been collected in error, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
  • No Sale: We do not sell the personal information of any user, including children.
  • Questions and Requests: You may contact us with questions or requests regarding privacy or your child's privacy.

📝 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the app, our practices, or applicable requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this public webpage with an updated "Last Updated" date.

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
