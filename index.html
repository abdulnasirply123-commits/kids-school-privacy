KIDS SCHOOL is built with children's safety and privacy as our highest priority. We do NOT collect, store, sell, or share personal information from our users. The app is safe, works offline for all learning content, and complies with Google Play Families Policies.

Welcome to KIDS SCHOOL, an educational application operated by [YOUR COMPANY OR DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you download and use our app on the Google Play Store.

🔍 Information We Collect

KIDS SCHOOL does NOT collect any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No Account Required: Children and parents can use the entire app without creating an account, registering, or logging in.
  • No Personal Details: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, ages, birthdates, phone numbers, photos, or physical addresses.
  • No Location Data: We do not access, track, or store your GPS or network location.
  • No Device Sensors or Media: We do not access your camera, microphone, photo gallery, or device files.
Note for the developer: If you ever add a feature that collects data (for example a login, contact form, or analytics SDK), you must describe it here and on the Google Play "Data safety" form. Keep this page accurate.

⚙️ How We Use Information

Since we do not collect any personal data, no personal information is stored, processed, or transmitted to any server.

  • Local Gameplay & Progress: Educational quiz scores, sound preferences, and language settings are stored purely locally on your device storage.
  • Offline Operation: All educational lessons, voice audios, pictures, and quizzes run directly on your device without requiring an active internet connection.
  • Ad Loads: An internet connection is used only when downloading advertisements. If the device is offline, the app continues to work normally and simply shows no ads.

🤝 Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any personal information with third-party companies, advertisers, or organizations. The only situations in which information may be shared are:

  • Distribution Platforms: Google Play Store (the app's distributor) processes standard installation, crash, and device information according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising Networks: Google AdMob serves advertisements in the app in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. See "Third-Party Services" below.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect the safety and rights of our users or the public.

We never disclose personal information for monetary or other valuable consideration unless you provide it to us voluntarily (for example, through a support email).

👶 Children's Privacy

KIDS SCHOOL complies strictly with international child privacy standards, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and the Google Play Designed for Families policy.

  • We do not knowingly collect or request personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • There are no online chat rooms, social media integrations, direct messaging, or communication channels with other users.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that any personal information has inadvertently been provided, please contact us immediately, and we will delete it promptly.

🔒 Data Storage and Security

We prioritize the security of your device. Because KIDS SCHOOL does not transfer personal data to external servers or cloud databases, there is no risk of your personal data being exposed in external server breaches. Local app preferences (such as sound on/off or language toggle) are stored safely within the app's isolated sandbox on your device.

For any information you choose to share with us directly (for example, via support email), we apply reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including secure storage and access limited to authorized personnel.

🧩 Third-Party Services & Advertising

  • AdMob Advertising: KIDS SCHOOL displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads are shown in safe, non-intrusive areas and never cover the learning content. Interstitial ads appear only occasionally between learning sessions.
  • No Personal Data for Ads: We do not collect any personal information to display ads. As required by Google's Designed for Families policy, ads shown to children are not personalized. Parents can control or reset ad personalization in the family's Google account settings.
  • No Behavioral Profiling by the App: KIDS SCHOOL itself does not perform behavioural profiling, user tracking, or sharing of usage data with ad networks.
  • Local Text-to-Speech: Voice pronunciations use your device's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine locally on the device.
